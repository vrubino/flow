Flow — Privacy policy

Flow is a personal app written for and used by its owner. It has no server and no analytics.

  • Your data stays in your Google account. Tasks, ideas and zones are stored on your device and in a hidden app-only folder in your own Google Drive (drive.appdata), used only to sync your devices.
  • Calendar. Flow reads and edits events in your Google Calendar (calendar.events) only to show your schedule and to add, change or delete appointments you create in Flow.
  • Nothing is shared or sold. No data is sent to the app's author or to any third party.
  • Revoking access: remove Flow at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.
