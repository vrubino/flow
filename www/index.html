How Flow works

Capture

  • Type, press Enter — it’s saved. Optional fields appear after; ignore them and keep going.
  • Plain text → Inbox. With a day → to-do. With a time → appointment (also added to Google Calendar). Start with idea: → idea. Tab changes the type before saving.
  • Review draft fri #simp ! · Call Alon tomorrow 15:00 · idea: SR with replay #project12
  • Dates: today, tomorrow, mon…sun, next week, in 3 days, 21/11 — or oggi, domani, venerdì, alle 15. ! priority, #name puts it in a zone — a few letters are enough (“#simp” → Simplicity paper).

Tasks that check themselves

  • Open a task to set a Done when… test and steps. Rows show progress, next step, idle days and postponements. Check in with Progress, Stuck or Done.

Zones

  • One per area of work. Each holds its tasks, ideas, links (Zoom, docs), copyable commands and notes. A zone also appears next to calendar events whose title contains its name or keywords, with its links one tap away.

Morning review

  • Sort the inbox, test due or stalled tasks, pick 3 focus tasks. Turn on a daily phone reminder from its last screen.
